# Antigravity instructions

Read and follow `AGENTS.md`, `ENFORCEMENT.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `docs/CURRENT-STATE.md`, and the relevant
standards under `docs/development/` before changing the project.

Antigravity/agy-specific temporary or diagnostic scripts must be placed under `scripts/agy/`. Do not write
scripts into the repository root, another CLI's directory, `database/`, `generator/`, or `tools/`.
